Help & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Medical Report Analyser prototype.

How does the biomarker extraction work?

MediPulse AI scans uploaded text and documents using fuzzy medical pattern matching and regular expressions to identify clinical biomarker names, detected numeric values, and units of measurement. It then matches each biomarker against clinical laboratory reference ranges.

Is my medical data kept private?

Yes. In this prototype, data analysis runs client-side inside your browser session. Your medical reports are not permanently stored on remote databases.

Can this tool replace a doctor?

No. MediPulse AI is designed strictly as an educational and informational tool to help patients understand complex lab terminology. It does not provide medical diagnoses or prescribe medications. Please always discuss your test results with a licensed healthcare professional.
